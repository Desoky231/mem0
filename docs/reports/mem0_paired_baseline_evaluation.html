Mem0 paired evaluation update

Run date: 27 July 2026 · DeepSeek V4 Flash · seed 42 · top-5 retrieval

This report replaces the earlier partial-session LoCoMo pilot. Both backends ingested all 19 dated sessions of conv-26 before answering the same 40 questions.

Important comparability note: these are results for this repository’s DeepSeek, session-level benchmark configuration—not a reproduction of the Mem0 paper. The paper uses GPT-4o-mini and incremental message-pair extraction with a rolling 10-message context, an asynchronously refreshed conversation summary, top-10 similar memories during updates, and a two-speaker timestamp-aware answer prompt. Do not compare the F1 values here directly with the paper’s published Mem0 scores.

Interpretation

  • Text memory was faster and stronger on this complete-conversation LoCoMo run.
  • Graph reduced stale-fact retrieval in controlled updates, but stale graph state and current-fact misses remained important failures.
  • Historical graph processing logged malformed deletion-extraction responses and search-tool errors during PersonaMem, although all 60 rows produced reports.
  • These are paired pilots, not a reproduction of published Mem0 results: the model, ingestion unit, extraction context, update retrieval depth, and answer prompt differ from the paper. More complete conversations and repeated seeds are needed before generalizing.
